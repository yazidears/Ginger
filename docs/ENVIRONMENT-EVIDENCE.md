# Heatwave and satellite evidence

The receptivity home now joins weather-based FFMC/ISI with a separate, explicit cell review priority. Its Satellite & heat review layer, alert list, cell explanation and cell API use the same evidence. This does not turn experimental rules into an ignition probability or an official warning.

- Heatwaves: Open-Meteo daily maximum temperatures, three days of modelled history and seven forecast days in Europe/Madrid. A run requires at least three consecutive days strictly above the local Meteocat threshold. Missing dates/temperatures break runs; missing thresholds remain unknown. Ongoing and forecast episodes are distinguished from past events. Temperatures are transferred from the assigned station's model grid point, not measured at each 200 m cell.
- Forecast fallback: when Open-Meteo is unavailable, MET Norway instantaneous temperatures and six-hour maxima wholly within each Madrid calendar day are combined with XEMA temperature samples for recent history. These are explicit lower bounds with incomplete daily coverage. Three positive exceedances can trigger review, but missing/subthreshold sampled peaks cannot prove no heatwave. Six-hour maxima crossing midnight are excluded.
- Thresholds: the 947 municipalities in Meteocat's [16 July 2026 CSV](https://static-m.meteo.cat/wordpressweb/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/16102355/LLindars_municipi_2026.csv), matched by municipality name from the geographic layer. Update the dated reference file when Meteocat publishes a replacement. No global arbitrary temperature threshold is substituted.
- Satellite thermal evidence: DeepFire first, explicitly labelled NASA FIRMS fallback. Original scalar source fields, instrument, acquisition time, FRP and cluster ID are retained and downloadable. These are processed fire-product records, not Level-1 radiance or raw imagery. Fresh detections within 5 km trigger review; within 1 km plus receptivity >=65 trigger urgent verification. Detections require acquisition within six hours and a feed fetched within 30 minutes. Duplicates, future acquisitions and stale feeds cannot trigger a thermal alert. The query includes a 5 km border around the map.
- Sentinel-2: actual reflectance-derived NDVI/NDMI from the existing raster pipeline. NDMI <0 with NDVI >=0.3, >=50% valid clear pixels, age <=10 days and weather receptivity >=65 triggers local fuel review. This is an experimental spectral signal, not calibrated live-fuel moisture. Stale or missing imagery adds no trigger.

FFMC/ISI remain physical weather-derived indices; heat and satellite evidence change investigation priority and explanations rather than applying invented multipliers. Future weather timeline steps do not forecast future satellite acquisitions: the evidence layer is explicitly current.

Source failures are classified without exposing provider response bodies or credentials. DeepFire network outages retain the fallback and show unavailable supplementary layers. An unavailable feed never establishes no fire.

Validation: `npx tsx scripts/receptivity/test-environment.ts`, `npm run test:receptivity`, `npm run typecheck:receptivity`, `npx tsx scripts/test-deepfire-context.ts`, `node scripts/test-provider-reliability.cjs`.
